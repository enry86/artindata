## Instagram caption
All 16,752 active satellites, placed where they were at 21:56 UTC on
15 October 2026. Two in three are Starlink, and 45% were launched since 2025.

The big Earth is wrapped in the low-orbit shell: 15,943 satellites within
2,500 km of the ground. The small Earth at the top right is drawn to scale, and
shows everything higher: navigation satellites at 20,000 km and the
geostationary ring at 36,000 km. The graph below counts satellites by altitude.
The white dot is the International Space Station.

Each tail shows where a satellite has just been: 30 seconds of travel on the big
Earth, 4 minutes in the to-scale view. Faster means longer.

Data: CelesTrak orbital elements, positions computed with SGP4 (public domain).
These are computed positions, not observations. Full method and code: link in bio.
