# Post text · Near, or like home

## Instagram caption
Two ways to look for a second Earth: the most Earth-like world, or the nearest one.

I scored 5,700 exoplanets on how much they resemble Earth (size, sunlight, star)
and placed each one twice: along a blue arm by similarity, along a violet arm by
distance. Gold marks the 12 most Earth-like, rose the 12 nearest.

The two lists share no world. The most Earth-like ones are hundreds of
light-years away. The nearest, Proxima b at 4.2 ly, is only #18.

The score is mine, inspired by the Earth Similarity Index, and the weights are an
editorial choice. Full method and code: link in bio.

Data: NASA Exoplanet Archive. Hollow marks: only a minimum mass is known.
